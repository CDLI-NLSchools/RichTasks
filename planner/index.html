CDLI Lesson Planner (Demo)

Seed a lesson from a topic, a rich task, or a question family. The planner builds the Gemini prompt; Gemini drafts the lesson; you bring the draft back.

Demo scope: topic and question seeds run on Mathematics 2201, the one course with a live topic map. The task seed runs on the Rich Tasks collection. Prompts use frame v2 (revised after the November 23, 2026 frame test). Assess items are specified as a draw in the lesson; composing them from the live bank stays in the Review Apps.
